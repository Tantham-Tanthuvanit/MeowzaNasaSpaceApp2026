# Codebase Modification Log — Home Map Zooms to 5,000%

**Timestamp**: 2026-11-14 09:44:24
**Author**: Claude Code (AI Assistant) & Wut
**Branch**: skyflip-ui-features
**Action**: Raised the Home sky map's zoom limit from 600% to 5,000%, with sharper SPHEREx tiles and finer grid lines as the map zooms in. Frontend only. Builds on `logs/2026-11-14_09-24-35.md`.

---

## 1. Summary of Changes
- **Zoom limit**: `MAX_ZOOM = 50` in `core/sky.js`, used by the wheel zoom and the zoom buttons. At 5,000% one map pixel is about one pixel of IRSA's sharpest SPHEREx tiles (order 5, about 13 arcseconds), so zooming further would only blur.
- **Sharper tiles**: the map now loads tile orders 1 to 5 (it stopped at 2). The sky is addressed on a finer grid (16,384 per face instead of 4,096) so the deepest tiles are read pixel for pixel. Where the ideal tile has not arrived, the sharpest one already loaded is shown, then the whole-sky preview.
- **Tile folders**: tiles numbered 10,000 and up live in `Dir10000` on IRSA; the loader now builds that path.
- **Finer grid**: grid lines are every 30° up to 250% zoom, then 15°, 5°, 2° and 1° (from 3,500%). Only the lines near the visible part of the sky are worked out. Zoomed out, the grid is the same as before.
- **New message**: "Loading a sharper picture…" shows while sharp tiles for the current view are downloading.

## 4. Known Limits
- A fast zoom shows a coarse, blocky picture for a second or two until the sharp tiles arrive.
- The hour labels still sit only on the Dec 0° line, so at high zoom there are usually grid lines but no labels. The pointer read-out gives the position.
- Sharp tiles are 30 to 140 KB each; a view needs up to about 40.
