## 1. Summary of Implementations
Following official **NASA SPHEREx mission architecture** and **WiseView (AMNH / Backyard Worlds)** discovery workflows, implemented the comprehensive 4-tier filtering suite:

### A. Visual & Image Enhancement Filters (WiseView Standard)
- **Invert / Negative Mode Toggle (`isInverted`)**: Added instant 1-click toggle button in [frontend/src/components/SkyViewer.jsx](file:///Users/Alpha/MeowzaNasaSpaceApp2026/frontend/src/components/SkyViewer.jsx) that flips the astronomical contrast to black sources on white background—the standard format used by discoverers for spotting faint moving dipoles.
- **Image Post-Processing Enhancement Toolbar**:
  - `Raw / Normal`: Unaltered science image
  - `Smooth (Gaussian)`: 3×3 Gaussian smoothing filter suppressing background Poisson noise and detector artifacts
  - `Sharpen (High-Pass)`: Unsharp mask filter emphasizing subtle point-source edges
  - `High Contrast`: Contrast stretch accentuating faint features above the background noise floor
