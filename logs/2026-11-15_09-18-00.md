## 1. Summary of Changes

- **`frontend/src/core/spectrum.js`**:
  - Created spectral feature catalogue defining known cosmic absorption lines (H₂O Ice at 3.05 µm, CH₄ Methane Overtone at 1.66 µm, CH₄ Fundamental at 3.30 µm, CO₂ Ice at 4.27 µm, atmospheric H₂O vapor at 1.40 & 1.90 µm, CO gas at 4.67 µm) and emission lines (Hydrogen Brackett-α at 4.052 µm, Paschen-α at 1.875 µm, Brackett-γ at 2.166 µm, PAH dust at 3.30 & 3.40 µm, shock-excited H₂ at 2.122 µm).
  - Added `wavelengthToColor(um)` helper mapping 0.75 – 5.00 µm to representative false-color shades across SPHEREx detector bands.
  - Implemented `findNearestFeature(um, maxDiff)` to identify features matching measured channels within tolerance.
  - Implemented `calculateContinuum(points)` running median filter for baseline continuum estimation.
  - Implemented `detectSpectralFeatures(points)` to identify significant absorption dips and emission peaks relative to continuum.
- **`frontend/src/core/spectrum.test.js`**:
  - Added unit test suite verifying feature catalogue boundaries, tolerance matching, band false-color mapping, continuum median smoothing, and dip/peak classification.
- **`frontend/src/components/SpectrumChart.jsx`**:
  - Upgraded spectrum panel into a full Absorption and Emission Spectra Graph positioned directly below the flipbook player.
  - Added interactive feature filters: `All features`, `Absorption (dips)`, `Emission (peaks)`, and `Clean plot`.
  - Added visual absorption guide markers (downward indicators) and emission guide markers (upward indicators) on the SVG plot with element/molecule labels.
  - Implemented Kirchhoff’s Dual Visual Spectra Bars: an Absorption Spectrum strip (continuous rainbow infrared gradient with dark absorption slits where light is absorbed) and an Emission Spectrum strip (dark space background with glowing vertical emission lines).
  - Added live point inspection readout on hover and keyboard navigation (arrow keys), detailing channel number, detector band, brightness, number of stacked pictures, and matching cosmic feature descriptions.
  - Added automated summary card of detected absorption dips and emission peaks for the active sky patch.
  - Added quick benchmark patch shortcuts (`WISE 0855` with strong methane & water ice absorption, and `Proxima Centauri`).
  - Added auto-load support for cached benchmark targets like `WISE 0855`.
- **`frontend/src/pages/Viewer.jsx`**:
  - Placed `SpectrumPanel` directly beneath the flipbook player and controls on all screen sizes (`max-lg:order-1`), preventing mobile layout re-ordering.
  - Passed `onSelectPatch` callback enabling direct patch navigation from the benchmark buttons.
  - Rendered panel even when no patch is selected yet, providing guidance and one-click access to benchmark targets.
- **`README.md`**:
  - Updated section 4 ("102-Channel Absorption & Emission Spectra Graph") and file map.
