# Add Newcomer Tutorial & Citizen Science Field Guide
**Timestamp**: 2026-11-14 16:38:00 +07:00
**Branch**: `restoring-old-features`

## Summary
Added an accessible 3-tab Tutorial & Field Guide modal (`TutorialModal`) so newcomers understand NASA SPHEREx infrared data, how to search and compare visits, how to interpret moving dipoles vs. variable stars, keyboard shortcuts, and how to flag candidates.

## Changes Made
- **[`frontend/src/components/icons.jsx`](file:///Users/Alpha/Desktop/MeowzaNasaSpaceApp2026/frontend/src/components/icons.jsx)**:
  - Added `BookIcon` for tutorial buttons.
- **[`frontend/src/components/TutorialModal.jsx`](file:///Users/Alpha/Desktop/MeowzaNasaSpaceApp2026/frontend/src/components/TutorialModal.jsx)**:
  - Created an accessible dialog (`role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, closes on `Escape` or backdrop click) matching SkyFlip's warm paper/ink theme with three interactive tabs:
    1. **1. What is SkyFlip?**: Plain-English overview of NASA SPHEREx, why cold brown dwarfs and Planet X candidates shine in infrared (3–5 µm), and why human eyes catch faint movers.
    2. **2. How to Hunt (4 Steps)**: Walkthrough of selecting a spot on the Sky Map, using Flipbook/Split view/What changed/Combined, inspecting Bands 1–6 & known stars, and flagging candidates.
    3. **3. Field Guide & Shortcuts**: Identification cards for Blue+Red dipole movers, Solid Red/Blue variable stars, and Pale Grey static stars, plus keyboard and map controls (`Space`, `←` / `→`, FOV, map drag/scroll).
  - Footer includes navigation between sections, a one-click link to launch the **5-step Guided Demo (WISE 0855)**, and **Got it, start exploring**.
