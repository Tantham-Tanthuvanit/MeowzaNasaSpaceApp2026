## 1. Summary of Changes
- **Why**:
  - The old demo was a card above the full viewer, so beginners still saw both side panels and every control.
  - Its text used jargon ("Band 6 · 4.42 µm", "dipole", "Shift-and-Add").
  - Its example could not show the change it described. In the cached Band 6 pictures, WISE 0855 moves about one SPHEREx pixel over the whole year and is barely brighter than the noise.
- **Finding a better example**: IRSA TAP and SIA answered 502 all evening, so no new spot could be loaded. Everything below used data already in Supabase, read only.
  - **Asteroids**: we checked every cached picture against IMCCE SkyBoT, around the centre and across whole wide fields.
    - Only asteroids of magnitude 18.6 and fainter were found, too faint for SPHEREx.
    - (66654) 1999 SF12 was checked by eye in the WISE 0855 18.5′ Band 1 pictures. Nothing is visible at its position.
  - **R Draconis** (a Mira variable, cached at 15′ Band 6): its measured centre brightness changes about 3×.
    - Each picture sees it at a different wavelength (4.46–4.84 µm), so the change is hard to explain honestly.
    - In every picture it is a saturated white blob.
  - **Barnard's Star**: the saved position is its year-2000 position. It has since moved about 4.5′ north, out of the cached 6.5′ field.
  - **Chosen: a streak.** In the cached WISE 0855 15′ view, a straight line crosses only the 2 May 2026 picture.
    - It appears in Bands 1 and 4 (same exposure time) and not in Bands 2 and 5, which were taken at other moments in that visit.
    - It is real and not a display artefact.
    - It is most likely a satellite; we can't tell which.
    - The line was measured on the stored pixels: from about (0, 71) to (137, 256) on the 0–256 grid. The circle sits at (73, 169), the line's point nearest the centre.
- **Noise-scaled difference pictures (new, default unchanged)**:
  - **The problem**: the default difference picture sets full colour at the 99.5th percentile of |difference|. Bright star leftovers set that scale, so the faint streak disappears into the grey.
  - **What `diff_scale=noise` does**: centres the colours on the median and sets full colour at 6× the robust standard deviation (median absolute deviation × 1.4826). The streak then shows as a clear red line.
  - **Kept separate**: it has its own cache key, and the default `peak` output is byte-for-byte unchanged.
  - **Where it is used**: only the guided demo.
- **Focus mode** (`?demo=1`–`5`): the Viewer returns `DemoFocus` instead of the three-column layout.
  - It shows the picture with a blue circle, a zoom toggle that zooms on the circle, and one step card with Back/Next and "Leave demo".
  - On phones the card sits under the picture with sticky Back/Next.
  - The heading gets focus when the step changes.
  - With reduced motion, the flipbook doesn't autoplay.
- **Steps**:
  1. A real photo of space.
  2. Flip through the pictures slowly and answer "In which picture does a line go through the circle?" (the answer is checked).
  3. The pictures without and with the line, side by side.
  4. The noise-scaled difference picture, with a colour key and a note that small red and blue dots are star leftovers.
  5. "You spotted something passing by": what a streak is, how a far-away world would look different, and buttons for a random patch, all the tools, or the advanced tour.
- **Asteroid slot**: `DEMO_ASTEROID` is ready for a real asteroid once IRSA is back. Filling it in switches the demo and its text to the asteroid version.
- **Advanced tour** (`?demo=a1`, `a2`): the old Combined and known-stars steps, rewritten in plain words, as a card above the full viewer on WISE 0855.
- **Entry points**: the nav, welcome page, sky map and How to use all use `DEMO_HREF()` and say "Take the 2-minute demo".
- **Random patch**: it now leaves demo mode (the `demo` parameter was kept before).
