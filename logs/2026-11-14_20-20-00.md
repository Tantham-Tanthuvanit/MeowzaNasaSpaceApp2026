# Add Right Ascension & Declination Grid to Sky Patch Viewer & Rename "What Changed" to "Difference Image"
**Timestamp**: 2026-11-14 20:20:00 +07:00  
**Branch**: `main`

## Summary
1. Added an interactive, dynamic Right Ascension (RA) and Declination (Dec) coordinate grid (graticule) overlay to the sky patch viewer with sexagesimal axis labels, border tick marks, and an astronomical cardinal orientation compass (`N ↑`, `E ←`), with toggle controls in the UI and keyboard shortcut (`G`).
2. Updated all buttons and view labels that switch to or describe the difference imaging mode from "What changed" to "Difference image".

## Changes Made
- **[`frontend/src/core/sky.js`](file:///C:/projects/MeowzaNasaSpaceApp2026/frontend/src/core/sky.js)**:
  - Added `DEC_STEPS_ARCSEC` and `RA_STEPS_SEC` candidate astronomical step tables.
  - Implemented `fmtDecGrid` and `fmtRaGrid` for formatting coordinates with clean sexagesimal degree/hour/minute/second units.
  - Implemented `skyPatchGrid(ra0, dec0, fov)` to calculate exact pixel positions and labels for lines of constant Declination and Right Ascension on a 256x256 sky patch with proper projection scaling and polar convergence compensation.
- **[`frontend/src/components/SkyPatchGrid.jsx`](file:///C:/projects/MeowzaNasaSpaceApp2026/frontend/src/components/SkyPatchGrid.jsx)**:
