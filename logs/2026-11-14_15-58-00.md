# Fix SkyMap Wheel Crash and Card Scrolling
**Timestamp**: 2026-11-14 15:58:00 +07:00
**Branch**: `restoring-old-features`

## Summary
Resolved a runtime `ReferenceError: MAX_ZOOM is not defined` in `SkyMap.jsx` that caused a white-screen crash when scrolling on the Sky Map or inside the "How do sky positions work?" card, and allowed overlay cards to scroll natively without being intercepted by map wheel zoom.

## Changes Made
- **[`frontend/src/components/SkyMap.jsx`](file:///Users/Alpha/Desktop/MeowzaNasaSpaceApp2026/frontend/src/components/SkyMap.jsx)**:
  - Imported `MAX_ZOOM` from `../core/sky`.
  - Updated `onWheel` to return early when `e.target.closest('[data-map-ui]')` is truthy so overlay panels (`CoordInfo`, `ProjectCard`, `PinCard`) can be scrolled with the mouse wheel or trackpad without triggering `e.preventDefault()` or zooming the map underneath.
- **[`frontend/src/components/MapCards.jsx`](file:///Users/Alpha/Desktop/MeowzaNasaSpaceApp2026/frontend/src/components/MapCards.jsx)**:
  - Adjusted `#coord-info` `max-h` from `max-h-[70vh]` to `max-h-[min(55vh,400px)]` so the popup fits comfortably inside the map container without clipping at the top.
