# Codebase Modification Log — Viewer Opens in One Band, and a Loading State That Shows Progress

**Timestamp**: 2026-11-14 10:55:40
**Author**: Claude Code (AI Assistant) & Wut
**Branch**: skyflip-ui-features
**Action**: The sky viewer no longer downloads all six bands for a first look, and the picture area shows what is happening while pictures load.

---

## 1. Summary of Changes
- **Cause of the slow first look**: the backend already downloads only the pictures the chosen light needs, but the viewer opened every spot in "All bands combined", which needs one picture from each of the 6 bands per visit (up to 24). One band needs one per visit (up to 4).
- **Default light**: a spot now opens in Band 1 (`DEFAULT_BAND` in `core/targets.js`; a project without Band 1 opens in its first band). "All bands combined" is still there and is now `?band=all` in the address. Its button says it needs six times the downloads. Links without `band` that used to show all bands now show Band 1.
- **Loading state** (`FlipPlayer`): replaces the single line of text. It names the light being loaded, shows one slot per picture (filled when it has arrived, pulsing for the ones downloading, outlined for the ones waiting; a bar when there are more than 24), and the count "N of M pictures are here". It also covers the old picture while the next light loads, so a picture is never shown under the wrong band label, and choosing a band reacts at once. The chosen band's dot pulses while it loads. Animations are off for people who ask for reduced motion.
- **No waiting between frames**: all frames of a flipbook are loaded when it opens, not one at a time as it plays.
- **One-picture bands**: some spots have a single picture in a band. The Light panel then says so and points to the other bands.
- No time estimate is shown: NASA's download speed varied too much during testing for one to be honest.
- No backend change.
