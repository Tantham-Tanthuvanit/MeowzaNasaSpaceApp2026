# Implement Judge Demo Mode (5-Step Guided Discovery Tour)
**Timestamp**: 2026-11-14 16:07:00 +07:00
**Branch**: `restoring-old-features`

## Summary
Added an interactive 5-step **Guided Demo Mode** (`?demo=1..5`) centred on the cached benchmark brown dwarf **WISE 0855** (`RA 08h55m10.831s, Dec -07°14′42.53″`), allowing judges and first-time visitors to experience every scientific feature of SkyFlip immediately with real NASA SPHEREx data.

## Changes Made
- **[`frontend/src/components/DemoTour.jsx`](file:///Users/Alpha/Desktop/MeowzaNasaSpaceApp2026/frontend/src/components/DemoTour.jsx)**:
  - Created the 5-step guided tour card with step pills, plain-English explanations, interactive "Try this" prompts, and Next/Previous/Exit controls:
    1. **Flipbook (Band 6 · 4.42 µm)**: Blinking long-wave infrared visits where cold brown dwarfs glow.
    2. **Split View**: Comparing earliest (`V1`) and latest (`V4`) SPHEREx survey visits side by side.
    3. **What Changed (Difference Image)**: Highlighting the blue-to-red dipole motion signature.
    4. **Shift & Add (Combined)**: Demonstrating co-addition along a trial motion vector for faint Planet X candidates.
    5. **Spectrum & Catalogues**: Enabling Gaia DR3 + AllWISE overlays and pointing to the 102-channel SPHEREx infrared spectrum.
