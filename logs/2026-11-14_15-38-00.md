## 1. Summary of Changes

- **Backend Multi-Pair Difference Imaging** (`backend/data_loader.py` & `backend/main.py`):
  - In `get_sky_patch`, pre-computes difference images across all combinations of survey visits (`a` and `b`), stores their signed URLs in `difference_images` dictionary (`{"0_1": url, "0_2": url, ...}`), and maintains backward-compatible `difference_image_url` for the full baseline.
  - Added new REST API endpoint `GET /api/difference-image` in `backend/main.py` allowing on-demand calculation of any `late_idx` minus `early_idx` visit pair at any FOV and stretch.
  - Added `DataLoader.get_difference_image` with bounds checking, chronological ordering, and validation against RGB composite bands.

- **Split View Player Component** (`frontend/src/components/SplitPlayer.jsx`):
  - Created a dedicated, responsive side-by-side dual-epoch viewer (`SplitPlayer.jsx`) with Inter & Space Grotesk styling.
  - Left and Right panels feature independent visit selectors (`V1`, `V2`, `V3`, `V4`), individual timestamp badges, target center reticle, and Gaia/WISE catalog and candidate flag overlays.
  - Full coordinate click-to-pick / click-to-flag support on either pane.
  - Quick action buttons to swap left/right panes (`⇄ Swap panes`) and jump directly to difference imaging (`Subtract in What changed →`).

- **Interactive Difference Image Pair Selector** (`frontend/src/pages/Viewer.jsx`):
  - Added Reference (Earlier) and Comparison (Later) visit `<select>` dropdowns in the "What changed" view.
  - Added quick preset buttons for full span (`V1 → V{N}`) and sequential visit steps (`V1 → V2`, `V2 → V3`, etc.).
  - Instant loading from pre-computed `differenceImages` map with transparent background fallback to `fetchDifferenceImage`.
  - Dynamic plain-language caption and bipolar colormap explanation updating to match the active subtraction.

- **Viewer Navigation & Mode Tabs** (`frontend/src/pages/Viewer.jsx`):
  - Expanded the "Show" button group from 3 to 4 pill options: `Flipbook`, `Split view`, `What changed`, and `Combined`.
  - Added dedicated informational panel and workflow shortcuts for Split View.

- **API & Documentation**:
  - Exported `fetchDifferenceImage` and exposed `differenceImages` map in `useFrames` hook (`frontend/src/services/api.js`).
  - Updated `README.md` to document Split View and custom difference pairs.
