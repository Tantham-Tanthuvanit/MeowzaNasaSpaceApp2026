## 1. Summary of Changes
Implemented an interactive **Side-by-Side Dual Epoch Comparison & Astrometric Difference Highlighter** in `frontend/src/components/SkyViewer.jsx`.

### Key Features Built:
1. **Dynamic Epoch Selectors**:
   - In Side-by-Side mode, users can pair any two survey epochs (e.g. Epoch 1 vs Epoch 4 for maximum 18-month baseline drift, or Epoch 2 vs Epoch 3).
2. **Multi-Mode Comparative Layouts**:
   - **Dual View (Side-by-Side)**: Synchronized side-by-side inspection of early vs late survey epochs with zoom, stretch, and filter controls.
   - **Trio + Diff Map**: Scientific 3-panel comparative layout displaying `[Epoch A]` | `[Difference Subtraction Dipole Map (B - A)]` | `[Epoch B]`.
   - **Curtain Wipe**: Interactive draggable before-and-after split slider (0% to 100%) to smoothly wipe between the two epochs.
3. **Astrometric Difference Highlighting & Motion Vectors**:
   - `Highlight Differences: ON/OFF` toggle button.
   - **Position A**: Glowing cyan pulsing reticle on the left epoch at `(coordsA)`.
   - **Ghost Reticle**: Dashed cyan ring on the right epoch indicating the target's starting position in Epoch A.
   - **Position B**: Glowing amber pulsing reticle on the right epoch at `(coordsB)`.
   - **Vector Arrow**: SVG dashed motion trajectory vector connecting position A to position B, indicating proper motion angle and direction.
   - **Scientific Telemetry Pill**: Real-time calculated drift distance in pixels, arcseconds, baseline months, and calculated annual proper motion in $\text{mas/yr}$.
   - **Dipole Detection in Difference Map**: Blue ring marking negative flux trough (shifted away) and red/amber ring marking positive flux peak (appeared).
4. **Interactive Flagging in Comparison Mode**:
   - Fully supports clicking on either panel to flag candidates with accurate astrometric coordinates.
5. **Database Benchmark Persistence**:
   - Updated `backend/database.py` with `INSERT OR IGNORE` to guarantee initial benchmark discoveries (Sedna, WISE 0855, Luhman 16) are always preserved.
