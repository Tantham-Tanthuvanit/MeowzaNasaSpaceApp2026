## 1. Explanation of Step 1

NASA's official *Planet X and SPHEREx* challenge resources released four critical benchmark test coordinates:
1. **90377 Sedna (Planet X Analog)**: An extreme detached Trans-Neptunian Object with an 11,400-year orbit whose alignment inspired the Planet Nine / Planet X hypothesis. Its slow orbital apparent motion (~1.5 arcsec/hr) tests outer solar system tracking.
2. **Luhman 16 (WISE J1049-5319)**: The closest brown dwarf system to Earth (6.5 light-years away) consisting of an L-dwarf and T-dwarf binary pair with dynamic atmospheric weather and strong 1–5 µm infrared variability.
3. **North Ecliptic Pole (NEP)**: SPHEREx's primary deep survey field with maximum orbital overlap and highest signal-to-noise ratio.
4. **South Ecliptic Pole (SEP)**: The southern deep calibration field for temporal stability.
