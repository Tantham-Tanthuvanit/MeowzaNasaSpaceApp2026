## 1. Summary of Changes
- **Core Celestial Math** (`frontend/src/core/sky.js`):
  - Added `randomSkyPoint()`: samples uniform random coordinates on the celestial sphere in equatorial coordinates ($0^\circ \le \text{RA} < 360^\circ$, $-85^\circ \le \text{Dec} \le 85^\circ$ to prevent singular pole projections), rounded to 4 decimals (~0.36 arcseconds precision).
- **Icons** (`frontend/src/components/icons.jsx`):
  - Added `ShuffleIcon` following the same SVG stroke, dimensions, and line styling as `PlusIcon` and `CloseIcon`.
- **Sky Map Home Page** (`frontend/src/pages/Home.jsx`):
  - Added a "Random patch" button (`btn-plain h-12 rounded-xl px-4 text-base`) directly adjacent to the "Search" button in the project toolbar.
  - When clicked (`pickRandomPatch`), it generates a random sky position, clears active search/project selections, resets zoom to 1 to show the whole sky context, centers the map view on the target, and drops a pin. This docks the `PinCard` containing "Watch this spot" and "Save as project".
- **Sky Viewer Page** (`frontend/src/pages/Viewer.jsx`):
  - Added a "Random patch" button (`btn-plain h-11 px-4 text-base`) next to "Show me" in the "Where in the sky?" form.
  - When clicked (`pickRandomPatch`), it generates a random sky position and calls `goTo(newRa, newDec)`, instantly populating the formatted inputs, updating the URL parameters, and fetching the SPHEREx frames.
- **Testing & Documentation**:
  - Added unit test suite in `frontend/src/core/sky.test.js` validating bounds, non-determinism, and sexagesimal format/parse round-trips.
  - Updated `README.md` to document the feature.
