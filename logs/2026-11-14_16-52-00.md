# Add Contamination Flags & SPHEREx Observation Metadata
**Timestamp**: 2026-11-14 16:52:00 +07:00
**Branch**: `metadata-and-contamination-flags`

## Summary
Added astronomical quality/contamination flag decoding (`cc_flags`, `ph_qual`, `ext_flg` for AllWISE; `ruwe`, `parallax` for Gaia DR3), artifact filtering in the Gaia/WISE overlay, and a live observation metadata strip (`Release`, `MJD`, `Exposures stacked`, `Calibration`) below the Flipbook player.

- **[`backend/catalog_service.py`](file:///Users/Alpha/Desktop/MeowzaNasaSpaceApp2026/backend/catalog_service.py)**:
  - Added `decode_wise_cc_flags(cc_flags)` to decode AllWISE `cc_flags` codes (`D`: Diffraction spike, `P`: Latent persistence, `H`: Scattered-light halo, `O`: Optical ghost, `0`: Clean).
  - Updated IRSA TAP queries for `gaia_dr3_source` (added `parallax`, `ruwe`, tagging `RUWE >= 1.4` as crowded/unresolved binary) and `allwise_p3as_psd` (added `cc_flags`, `ext_flg`, `ph_qual`), returning `is_contaminated` and `contamination_note` on every catalog source.
