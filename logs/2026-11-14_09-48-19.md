**Timestamp**: 2026-11-14 09:48:19

**Action**: Raised the Home sky map's zoom limit from 5,000% to 20,000%. The last step is magnification only: the SPHEREx tiles have no more detail past 5,000%. Frontend only. Builds on `logs/2026-11-14_09-44-24.md`.

---

## 1. Summary of Changes
- **Zoom limit**: `MAX_ZOOM = 200` in `core/sky.js`. From 5,000% to 20,000% each pixel of the sharpest tile is drawn up to 4 screen pixels wide, as plain blocks with no smoothing.
- **Grid**: two finer steps, every 0.5° from 7,000% and every 0.25° from 14,000%. Lines now run to 89.9° (they stopped at 88°, which left the poles empty at high zoom). RA lines are spaced wider towards the poles so they do not pile up there. Below 250% zoom the grid is the same as before.
